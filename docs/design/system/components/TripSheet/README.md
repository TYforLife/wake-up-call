The sheet over the map while riding: where you get off, how long, and that the alarm is set.

- Order is fixed: "Get off at" (`muted`), the stop (`stop`), time and distance (`figure`), the alarm line, one `ghost` button.
- GPS lost: the figures turn `muted` with their age, and the alarm line turns `danger` and says when it will ring at the latest.
- Waiting for the next bus: the eyebrow reads "Off at <stop> · next stop" and the figure line "Waiting for bus 2".
