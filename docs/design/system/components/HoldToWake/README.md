The alarm screen: the only loud screen. Night palette in both themes.

- `alarm-title` "Wake up", then where (`alarm-ink`), then how it's ringing (`alarm-muted`: "Buzzing · phone was on Mute").
- The hold target fills the lower half (`hold-target`, `radius-xl`) so a groggy thumb can't miss it; it fills in `alarm-amber` over 1 s of holding. A tap, a brush or a short press does nothing.
- Second ways to stop it, written under the target: hold volume down, or the notification button.
- The While you slept panel, when there is one, sits between the title and the target and scrolls on its own.
