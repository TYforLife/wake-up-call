Setting a stop's circle on the map: where it will ring.

- The size in `number-xl`, the presets as chips (Auto, 150, 250, 400, 700 m), the slider on a square-root scale (50 m–2 km; most of the track under 1 km).
- The circle on the map follows live; **Save** applies it to a trip that's running too.
- Auto says what it means: "Auto · about 310 m, from your past rides".
